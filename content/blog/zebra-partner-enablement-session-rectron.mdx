Tyflex attended a **Zebra partner enablement session hosted at Rectron South Africa**, working through the current Zebra hardware range hands-on with the distributor's team.

## What it covered

- **Enterprise mobile computers and scanners** — rugged handhelds and imagers for warehouse, retail and field use, and how to match device class to duty cycle.
- **RFID** — fixed and handheld readers, and the practical first use cases: asset registers, returnable containers, work-in-progress on a single line.
- **Industrial and mobile printing** — pairing printers with the right media and encoding for labelling at the point of work.
- **Support and warranty** — service coverage and repair paths through the distributor.

## Where this fits for us

It sits alongside the TSC and Printronix printing we already supply, broadening what we can specify and support across enterprise mobility and scanning. For most customers the value is in getting the device, the accessories and the software integration right the first time — and having a clear support path when a unit needs service.
