Tyflex was at **AWS Summit Johannesburg** this year, with our venture **Vekta** on the ground. As an AWS Partner, the Summit is where we keep our cloud practice current — new services, reference architectures, and how other teams on the continent are actually running workloads.

<YouTube youtubeId="EbnNNRx1wEY" label="Vekta at AWS Summit 2026, Johannesburg" />

## What we took from it

- **Migration keeps getting more boring — in a good way.** The tooling for moving databases and servers to AWS has matured to the point where the hard part is planning and validation, not the cut-over itself. That matches what we see on our own projects.
- **Security belongs at the foundation.** The strongest sessions were about landing zones, guardrails and identity set up once, up front, rather than bolted on later.
- **AI is a workload, not a bolt-on.** The teams getting value are starting from one focused use case and a governed account structure, not a big-bang rollout.
