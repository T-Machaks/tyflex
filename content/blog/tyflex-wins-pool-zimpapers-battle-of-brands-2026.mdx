The Tyflex pool team brought home **1st position** at the 4th Edition of the **Zimpapers Battle of Brands**, held on **24 October 2026** — a sporting and social event that brings together teams from dozens of Zimbabwean companies, including Delta Corporation, Fidelity Life, ZETDC, Parliament of Zimbabwe, and fellow competitors across pool, football and other disciplines.

## A result worth celebrating

Battle of Brands is as much about teamwork and company culture as it is about the scoreboard, and this year our team came out on top of the pool table, picking up the trophy and medals to show for it. Big congratulations to everyone who represented Tyflex on the day — the result reflects the same focus and preparation we bring to our day job.

## Why we take part

Events like this are a good reminder that the people behind Tyflex's cloud, networking and infrastructure work are the same people who show up for each other outside the office. We'll be back to defend the title at the next edition.

Thanks to Zimpapers and all the sponsors and participating brands for putting together another well-run Battle of Brands.
