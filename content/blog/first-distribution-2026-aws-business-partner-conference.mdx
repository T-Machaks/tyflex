Tyflex attended the **First Distribution 2026 AWS Business Partner Conference** at Zebula Golf Estate & Spa on 25–26 March 2026, powered by AWS. First Distribution — part of Epsidon Technology Holdings, and the team behind First for Cloud — is one of Africa's principal AWS distributors.

## What it covered

- **AWS partner programme updates** — how the programme is evolving and what it means for partners delivering to small and mid-sized businesses.
- **Go-to-market for African SMBs** — packaging cloud solutions so smaller organisations can adopt them without a large in-house technical team.
- **Funding and incentive programmes** — the migration and proof-of-concept support available to move workloads onto AWS.
- **Technical enablement** — current reference architectures for migration, backup and recovery, and security foundations.
